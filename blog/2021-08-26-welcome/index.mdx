This blog is for updates, reflections, and notes from my maker journey.

{/* truncate */}

I use the main Projects section for complete project documentation and the Docs section for tutorials and reusable references. The blog is a more flexible place for writing about what I am exploring, what I learned, and what changed while building.

Topics here may include embedded systems, power electronics, fabrication, open-source work, lab experiments, and project updates that do not need to become full documentation pages.

The focus will stay practical: what I tried, what worked, what failed, and what I want to improve next.
